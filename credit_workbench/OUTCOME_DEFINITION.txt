================================================================================
PRE-REGISTRATION: OUTCOME DEFINITION AND BASELINES
ALM / refinancing-risk screen for Indian NBFCs
================================================================================
Written:  2026-10-12
Status:   Fixed BEFORE the pre-event extraction was inspected. The point of
          writing this first is that it cannot be edited to fit whatever the
          numbers turn out to say.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRIMARY OUTCOME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A publicly documented failure to pay a debt obligation on its due date,
occurring within 12 months of the OBSERVATION DATE.

Observation date = the publication timestamp of the annual report, not the
fiscal year-end. These differ by 3-6 months and the difference is the whole
question.

Qualifying evidence, in order of preference:
  (a) exchange disclosure of a missed interest or principal payment
  (b) rating action to D / RD with default cited
  (c) debenture-trustee notice of default
  (d) regulator-documented payment failure

Not qualifying as PRIMARY: share-price decline, auditor emphasis of matter,
promoter pledge invocation, management change, or press speculation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SECONDARY OUTCOMES - LABELLED SEPARATELY, NEVER POOLED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
S1  Rating downgrade of two or more notches within 12 months
S2  Rating watch / outlook to negative with liquidity cited
S3  Documented refinancing difficulty: failed issue, CP rollover failure,
    or disclosed reliance on emergency credit lines
S4  Regulatory intervention: board superseded, moratorium, business
    restriction
S5  Formal resolution: IBC / NCLT admission or RBI-directed resolution

Week one pooled these. That was wrong and is the reason CLAIM 4 in the
feasibility note was marked "conflates event types". S4 and S5 can arrive
years after the economics have already failed, which inflates apparent lead
time; S1 and S2 are a rating agency's opinion, so predicting them is partly
predicting an analyst, not an institution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EVENT DATES USED, AND WHICH OUTCOME CLASS EACH IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DHFL              2019-06-04   PRIMARY   missed NCD interest payment
  IL&FS group       2018-09-04   PRIMARY   CP default (via ITNL filings)
  Reliance Home Fin 2019-09-01   PRIMARY   payment default, rating D
  SREI Infra        2021-10-04   S4        RBI superseded board
  Reliance Capital  2021-11-29   S4        RBI superseded board

Only three of the five are PRIMARY events. SREI and Reliance Capital enter
the analysis as S4 and must be reported separately. The headline count is
therefore n=3, not n=5.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CENSORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A survivor observation counts as a clean negative only if the institution
survived 12 months past the observation date with no PRIMARY event. An
institution still trading at the end of the sample with less than 12 months
elapsed is censored and dropped, not counted as a negative.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. BASELINES, FIXED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ALM measure must beat ALL of these on incremental information, not merely
be associated with distress:

  B1  equity / total assets                (beat the FDI on US held-out data)
  B2  the existing Fundamental Distress Index
  B3  total leverage (assets / equity)
  B4  share of borrowings maturing <= 1 year, from the balance sheet alone
      -- NOT from the maturity ladder. This is the baseline that matters:
         if a single line already available in the accounts does the job,
         parsing the ten-bucket table adds nothing.
  B5  rating level at the observation date, where recoverable

ALM measure as specified: cumulative inflow minus cumulative outflow through
the 1-year bucket, divided by total assets, computed within basis
(contractual compared only with contractual).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. STOP CONDITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do not expand to a 100-institution panel unless BOTH hold:
  (i)  a pre-event observation with a verified publication timestamp earlier
       than the event is recoverable for at least 3 PRIMARY cases, and
  (ii) matched contemporaneous survivor observations are recoverable on the
       same bucket schema and the same basis.

If the comparison can only be made across different bases or different bucket
schemas, the measure is not yet computable and the panel should not be built.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE HYPOTHESIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- B4 separates failures from survivors as well as the ladder-based gap.
- Failed firms disclosed the BEHAVIOURAL basis pre-event while survivors
  disclosed contractual, making the gap a reporting-choice artefact rather
  than a risk measure.
- The pre-event gap for failures sits inside the survivor range.
- Pre-event tables are standalone shells (the SREI problem) for most failures,
  so the measured entity is not the entity that failed.
