================================================================================
NBFC CREDIT WORKBENCH - HOW TO USE IT
================================================================================
For: a credit analyst covering Indian non-bank lenders.
Companion documents: ARCHITECTURE.txt (what it is and why),
                     LOW_LEVEL_DESIGN.txt (how it is built),
                     PRODUCT.txt (what is built, what is not, and the limits).


================================================================================
1. STARTING IT
================================================================================
    python3 -m http.server 8777 --directory credit_workbench

Then open  http://localhost:8777/workbench.html

The data file loads as a sibling script, so opening the HTML directly from
the filesystem (file://) will NOT work - the page will sit on "Loading
workbench data".

A published copy opens from its own link with no server. Everything works
there except the links to the generated text files, which are relative paths;
use "Show plain text" or "Copy as text" instead.


================================================================================
2. THE ONE CONTROL THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING
================================================================================
The blue bar at the top reads EVIDENCE AS IT STOOD ON, with a date box, a
slider, and four presets.

Move it and the entire page rebuilds as the file read on that date. Findings
dated later are not hidden, they are not computed. The recorded outcome for an
issuer is withheld until the date passes it, so you cannot accidentally read
the answer before the question.

This is what the tool is for. A dashboard tells you what is true now. This
tells you what was knowable then - which is the only way to judge whether a
monitoring process was adequate, as opposed to whether a failure looks obvious
in hindsight.

Two ways to use it:

  LIVE MONITORING   leave it at today. The rail is your reading queue.
  PROCESS REVIEW    set it to the date you made a decision, and read the file
                    as it stood. Then move it forward and watch what arrived.


================================================================================
3. THE ISSUER RAIL IS A QUEUE, NOT A RANKING
================================================================================
The left column lists all 16 issuers sorted by how many high-severity findings
sit unexamined in their file. The dots are the severity mix; the number is the
count of substantive findings.

READ IT AS: "where should I spend this afternoon".
NEVER AS:   "which of these is riskiest".

The number is a count of things to look at. Repco Home Finance - a small,
conservative lender that was never in difficulty - carries 14 findings over
its full history. Bajaj Finance carries 4. The gap between them is mostly how
much each one filed, not how sound each one is.


================================================================================
4. THE SIX TABS, AND WHEN EACH IS THE RIGHT ONE
================================================================================

FINDINGS            Start here. Grouped high / medium / low, newest first
                    within each group. Every card has the same five parts:

                      WHAT      what changed, with the figures
                      WHY       the mechanism, stated as a hypothesis
                      AGAINST   the innocent explanation, where one exists
                      NEXT      what you do about it
                      EVIDENCE  the source file, its timestamp, and a link

                    Read AGAINST before you act on WHAT. It is there because
                    most findings have a boring explanation, and the tool
                    cannot tell which.

CAPITAL & LEVERAGE  Annual balance-sheet history: equity/assets, leverage,
                    borrowings/assets, ROA, gross NPA. Each row is dated by
                    when the ANNUAL REPORT was published, not by the fiscal
                    year end - three to six months earlier, and the difference
                    that invalidated three of five cases in the research.
                    Rows marked "est" had no report matched and carry a
                    conservative date; treat their lead times as optimistic.
                    Equity and total assets sit beside the ratio so you can
                    check it rather than trust it.

REPORTED FIGURES    Quarterly income, PBT, PAT and impairment exactly as
                    filed, standalone and consolidated kept separate. The
                    FILING LAG bar is the thing to look at: the tick marks the
                    SEBI limit and the red portion is the overrun.

FILED STATEMENT     Any single filing rendered as the profit and loss account
                    it represents, with the original XBRL tag beside every
                    line. Use it to verify a number before you rely on it.
                    Underneath, six relationships that hold by definition of a
                    P&L are recomputed and reported as holds / BREAKS / not
                    checked.

DISCLOSURE TIMELINE Every exchange announcement, newest first, with rating
                    actions and clarification requests marked and links to the
                    original PDFs. Use it when you need the sequence.

INVESTIGATION BRIEF The prose summary, for pasting into a credit memo. It
                    states what changed, why it may matter, what contradicts
                    it, the questions for the analyst, and - at the end - what
                    it cannot tell you. It does not conclude. That is
                    deliberate.

5. A WORKED INVESTIGATION
================================================================================
Question: what could an analyst have known about Reliance Capital before the
RBI superseded its board on 29 November 2021?

STEP 1  Set the date to 2019-05-31 (a preset). The rail now reads:

            RHFL           7 high
            RELCAPITAL     6 high
            PNB HOUSING    3 high
            DHFL           2 high

        Reliance Home Finance and Reliance Capital are first and second. Both
        failed. PNB Housing is third and survived - the queue is not a
        prediction.

STEP 2  Open RELCAPITAL. The outcome banner says a recorded outcome exists
        AFTER this date and is withheld. Good: you are reading blind.

STEP 3  Findings tab. The earliest high-severity items are rating actions
        from 29 March, 19 April and 26 April 2019 - 976, 955 and 948 days
        before the intervention. Read the WHAT text: the company's own
        filings call the agencies' actions "completely unjustified and
        inappropriate". A borrower publicly disputing two rating agencies
        within a week is the thing to notice.

STEP 4  The 26 April finding is a DISCLOSURE BURST - four rating filings in
        the 30 days from 29 March. No surviving issuer in this universe
        exceeds two.

STEP 5  Capital & leverage tab. Equity/assets fell from 18.6% (FY2017) to
        1.2% (FY2018), knowable 2018-09-20. Reserves went from Rs 15,081
        crore to Rs 760 crore on a Rs 4,556 crore loss. Leverage for the
        later years reads "n/m" because equity is negative and the ratio
        inverts - read borrowings and the obligations falling due instead.

STEP 6  Move the date to 2019-10-11. A new high finding appears: the issuer
        has told the exchange its debt servicing "will be delayed", naming
        five ISINs. That is 25.6 months before the intervention, and it is
        the issuer's own admission, not an opinion or a ratio.

STEP 7  Move to 2020-12-09. The disclosures ESCALATE: having said payment
        would be delayed, the company now states it was not made.

STEP 8  Investigation brief tab. Copy it into your memo. Then read the last
        paragraph, which tells you what the brief cannot establish.

At 2021-06-30, five months before the event, the rail reads RELCAPITAL 17
high, RHFL 15, DHFL 12 - the three that failed, in order, above everything
else. That is a demonstration, not evidence: three cases, chosen knowing how
they ended, in a universe of sixteen.


================================================================================
6. HOW TO READ EACH KIND OF FINDING
================================================================================

MISSED FILING DEADLINE      The strongest signal in this corpus, and the only
                            one that does not depend on the reported figures
                            being true. What matters is not lateness but how
                            late against the issuer's OWN habit. DHFL filed
                            its audited FY2019 result 114 days after period
                            end against a 60-day limit; RHFL 136 days against
                            its own ~50-day norm. Most low-severity ones are a
                            few days over and routine - 57 of 124 in the first
                            build were noise.

DISCLOSED PAYMENT FAILURE   The issuer telling the exchange it did not pay, or
                            would be late. Read the quoted sentence, not the
                            filing's title: the titles are identical whether a
                            company paid or did not.

FIGURES DO NOT RECONCILE    A year-to-date figure that does not equal the sum
                            of its quarters. Means something was restated
                            after it was first published. Often innocent - a
                            merger restating comparatives, as with Shriram -
                            but you should know which.

CAPITAL EROSION             Equity/assets falling 25% or turning negative. The
                            only detector with zero false positives across 49
                            issuer-years of healthy issuers, but it caught
                            only one event in eight and caught it late.

MATERIAL DETERIORATION      A swing into loss, or credit costs up 50%+ year on
                            year. A fall in profit is deliberately NOT
                            reported; only an actual loss is.

RATING ACTION /             A third party, or the exchange, getting worried.
EXCHANGE CLARIFICATION      Useful for dating when a concern became public.
                            Only notices whose text matches distress language
                            are promoted; routine ones stay on the timeline.

AUDITOR OPINION             Anything other than unmodified. The most direct
                            evidence that the figures should not be taken at
                            face value.

NO CONSOLIDATED ACCOUNTS    The issuer files standalone only. For a subsidiary
                            inside a group this is the SREI problem: the
                            listed entity's accounts need not describe the
                            business carrying the risk. For a lender with no
                            subsidiaries it means nothing.

EVIDENCE GAP / STALE /      NOT about the issuer. These describe the limits of
INCONSISTENT SCALE          what can be known, and are listed separately for
                            that reason.

7. THE MISTAKES THIS TOOL MAKES EASY, AND HOW TO AVOID THEM
================================================================================

READING THE COUNT AS RISK
    It is a reading queue. Repco at 14 is not riskier than Bajaj at 4.

READING ZERO FINDINGS AS SAFETY
    SREI Infrastructure has no payment findings because 51 of its 57
    disclosure attachments are scanned images that cannot be read. There is an
    explicit coverage finding saying so. Silence from a document nobody can
    read is not evidence of calm. Always check the Limits of the evidence
    section before concluding an issuer is quiet.

TRUSTING A FIGURE WITHOUT OPENING IT
    Use the Filed statement tab. Every line carries its XBRL tag and the
    source link.

READING A LEAD TIME OFF AN ESTIMATED DATE
    19 of 176 annual rows have a publication date that was estimated, not
    matched to a report. They are marked "est" and their lead times are
    optimistic.

TREATING A CONFIRMATION AS A WARNING
    DHFL's SEBI default form is dated four days AFTER its default. It
    confirms, it does not predict. Check the finding's date against the event.

ASSUMING THE GROUP IS THE ISSUER
    Reliance Home Finance was a subsidiary of Reliance Capital. A finding on
    one is not a finding on the other. The tool records two such pairs by
    hand; it has no general entity resolution, which is its largest
    structural gap.


================================================================================
8. WHAT IT WILL NOT DO
================================================================================
- It will not tell you whether the reported figures are true. Reliance Capital
  reported the best one-year liquidity cover in its peer group while Rs 5,103
  crore of its debentures had already been recalled.
- It has no rating level or history. NSE's rating endpoint returns nothing;
  only rating ANNOUNCEMENTS are available, as free text.
- It covers no unlisted entity. IL&FS Financial Services, which defaulted on
  the commercial paper, filed nothing to any exchange.
- Structured quarterly results exist only from about 2019. DHFL has 14
  readable filings against 73 result filings listed.
- It gives no score, and that is deliberate: on this data a nine-component
  weighted index did not beat plain equity/assets.
- Sixteen issuers. India has over 9,000 registered NBFCs.

9. KEEPING IT CURRENT
================================================================================
    python3 ingest.py                      new filings and announcements
    python3 attachments.py                 new payment-disclosure PDFs
    python3 annual.py                      annual layer
    python3 build.py                       rebuild the workbench data
    python3 check_pit.py                   MUST report 0 violations
    python3 export_statements.py           regenerate the readable statements

Then wrap the JSON for the page:

    python3 -c "import json;d=json.load(open('workbench_data.json'));\
open('workbench_data.js','w').write('window.WB='+json.dumps(d,\
separators=(',',':'),default=str)+';')"

Every step is resumable and skips what it already has. To add an issuer,
append to ISSUERS in universe.py and run ingest for that symbol, then build.
