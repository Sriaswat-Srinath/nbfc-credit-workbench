Built on NSE primary filings: the corporate-announcements stream, the financial-results index, and the XBRL instance behind each result filing. Every figure shown is traceable to the filing that produced it and the timestamp at which that filing was disseminated. There is no composite risk score in this tool by design — on this data a nine-component index did not beat equity/assets, so the tool ranks evidence and leaves the judgement to the analyst.
