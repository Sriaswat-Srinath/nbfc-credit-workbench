Both axes are percentile ranks among institutions that have both analyses, 100 = riskiest. On the diagonal, the accounts and the market agree.

How to read this

  • The index is a rank, not a probability. 88 means worse than 88% of comparable institutions that year — not an 88% chance of failing. Most institutions scoring above 80 will not fail.
  • The implied PD is a model output, not a forecast. It assumes lognormal asset value, one debt maturity and no parent or state support. Its movement is far more trustworthy than its level.
  • A long-standing alert is not a warning. If an institution has been lit up for five years, the model is describing a weak balance sheet, not anticipating an event. The deterioration column is where the timing lives.
  • The accounts can only show what was reported. Yes Bank's profitability ranked mid-pack until FY2019 because the bad loans were not yet recognised. That is exactly the gap the market column is there to cover.
  • No market read means no second opinion, not safety. Two thirds of these institutions are unlisted or too thinly traded to solve, and the model is blind to state-backed banks whose prices embed an implicit guarantee.
  • Validated on 18 events, 13 of them PCA placements. Recall inside two years is 14 of 18 (95% CI 52–94%). On seven held-out events a rule that never saw them caught all seven, p = 0.0003. Four known failures remain unevaluable.
